Colours: a colourful hand-drawn picture: black ink lines filled with soft watercolour colour on cream paper. Most things in the picture are coloured in their own natural colours: furniture in warm wood and soft blue, clothes and cushions in sage green, dusty pink, mustard and terracotta, plants green, screens softly lit, water light blue; the wall and floor get a light warm wash, never left plain grey. Colours are gentle and harmonious like a picture book, never neon, never harsh, never a dark background. The main character stays black with green eyes, orange cheek marks and an orange tail flame.
