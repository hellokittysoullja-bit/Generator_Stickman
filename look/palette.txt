Colours: black ink lines and soft grey washes on cream paper, in exactly the hand-drawn style of the style references. Soft watercolour colour goes only on a few things that matter in the picture, the main object and one or two details, each in its own natural muted colour (a pink brain, a red heart, a green plant, an orange alarm clock, warm lamp light), while walls, floors and everything else stay ink and grey; never neon, never a fully coloured background. The main character stays black with green eyes, orange cheek marks and an orange tail flame.
