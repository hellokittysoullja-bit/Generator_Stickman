Colours: black ink lines and soft grey washes on plain cream paper, like the style reference. Soft watercolour colour goes only on one or two things that matter in the picture, the main object and maybe one detail, each in its own natural muted colour (blue water, a green plant, a pink cushion, a red heart, a yellow lamp light), and not everything in the same orange; furniture, clothes, walls, floors and everything else stay ink and light grey. Some of the things are coloured, never all of them. The background always stays the plain cream paper, never a coloured wall or a tinted background. Never neon, never harsh. The main character stays black with green eyes, orange cheek marks and an orange tail flame.
