# Instagram Reels distribution and ranking in 2025–2026, and what a "stuck at 100–200 views" pattern means

Research date: 07.11.2026. Labels used below: **[OFFICIAL]** means Instagram, Meta or Mosseri said it (sometimes quoted through press). **[MARKETER]** means a third-party blog or vendor. **[OLDER]** means before 2025.

## 1. What Mosseri and Instagram have officially said about Reels ranking signals (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
Officially, three signals matter most: **watch time, likes per reach and sends (DM shares) per reach**. Sends weigh slightly more for non-followers ("unconnected" reach) and likes slightly more for followers. On top of that sit eligibility filters: originality and reposts, watermarks, low quality, majority-text videos, Recommendation Guidelines, and (since 31.08.2026) unlabeled AI people. Skip rate is a **metric** in Insights. Instagram has **not** confirmed it as a ranking signal. Many popular claims (sends weigh "3–5×" likes, skip rate as a July 2026 ranking update) have no official source.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, 22.01.2025] Mosseri:** "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends. So when looking at your insights, pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach." Also: "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." — [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance); [ZoomSphere](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach); [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- **[OFFICIAL] Mosseri on watch time:** Instagram does not want "to penalize longer videos, which is why we look at not only the percentage of a video that was watched, but also the number of seconds." — [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance) (secondary quote)
- **Myth check:** the claim that sends carry "3 to 5 times the weight of likes" is a third-party estimate. Mosseri's actual wording is "slightly more important". A July 2026 "skip rate becomes a ranking signal" update has no Instagram or Mosseri source. Neither do the claims "original content gets 40–60 % more distribution", "shadowban update", "hashtags boost reach" or invented watch-time thresholds. — [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- **[OFFICIAL, OLDER — 31.05.2023] "Instagram Ranking Explained" (Mosseri):**
  - On Reels, "the majority of what you see is from accounts you don't follow."
  - The most important predictions are "how likely you are to reshare a reel, watch a reel all the way through, like it, and go to the audio page."
  - Signals in rough order: the viewer's activity; the viewer's history with the poster; information about the reel (audio, visuals, popularity); information about the poster.
  - Instagram makes these reels less visible to non-followers: "low-resolution or watermarked reels, reels that are muted or contain borders, reels that are majority text," and "reels that have already been posted on Instagram."
  - — [Instagram blog](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **[OFFICIAL, OLDER — 2022, updated 25.04.2023] Creators blog on eligibility for recommendations:**
  - Content that breaks the Recommendation Guidelines still reaches followers but is not recommended to non-followers.
  - Repeated violations, or a profile photo or bio that breaks the guidelines, can make the **whole account** ineligible for a period of time.
  - Practices: post original content; don't reupload reels already on Instagram; no watermarks; high-resolution 9:16 with no borders; text must not cover most of the screen; keep reels ≤ 3 min to be eligible for recommendation (now superseded, see section 3); "hook viewers in the first 3 seconds"; account must be public.
  - Account Status shows whether content is ineligible to be recommended to non-followers.
  - — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- **[OFFICIAL, OLDER — April 2024] Originality update:**
  - Recommended reposts are replaced with originals.
  - Aggregator posts are removed from recommendations.
  - The aim is to "correct" ranking so that smaller accounts can expand their reach more easily.
  - Mosseri described staged expansion: the top-performing reels are shown to a slightly wider audience, then the best of those to an even wider one, and so on.
  - — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)
- **[OFFICIAL, 30.04.2026] Originality rules extended to photos and carousels:**
  - Accounts that repost without meaningful edits are not recommended to non-followers. Followers still see their posts.
  - Changing a video's speed, or adding a screenshot with the original author's name, is not a big enough edit.
  - Edits that count as original: "unique text, creative edits, and voiceover", humor, commentary.
  - — [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator)
  - Per the tracker, recommendations return once most of the last 30 days' posts are original, and no numeric threshold is published. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- **[OFFICIAL] Other dated changes:**
  - 06.08.2025: reposts launch.
  - 10.12.2025: "Your Algorithm" launches in Reels (US first). Viewers see their inferred interest topics, can add, remove and adjust them, and recommended Reels show which topic drove them. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/10/instagrams-new-your-algorithm-tool-gives-you-more-control-over-the-reels-you-see); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-will-let-you-control-which-topics-its-algorithm-recommends-133002612.html)
  - 18.12.2025: hashtags capped at 5 per post.
  - 16.04.2026: Your Algorithm reaches Explore.
  - 10.06.2026: Your Algorithm reaches Feed.
  - 31.08.2026: unlabeled profiles of AI-generated people lose recommendation eligibility.
  - 21.10.2026, Meta Q3 call: Reels now rank *sequences* rather than single clips; nearly 45 % of recommended Reels are under a day old; relevance launches raised Reels time spent by over 4 %.
  - — [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/) (compiled timeline; primary sources are named on that page, I did not check each one)
- **[OFFICIAL] Trial Reels (launched 10.12.2024):**
  - Shown only to non-followers.
  - Not in followers' Feed or Reels tab, and off the profile grid until shared.
  - After the test, the creator shares the reel with everyone or archives it.
  - — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2024/12/10/instagrams-new-trial-reels-feature-lets-creators-test-content-with-non-followers); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-launches-trial-reels-audience-sampling/735136/)
  - Mosseri later opened it to all public accounts with ≥ 1,000 followers and said Instagram is considering smaller accounts. — [The Keyword](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/instagram-expands-trial-reels-to-all-public-creators-with-1k-or-more-followers)
  - Sources disagree on the window (24–72 h) and on eligibility (200 vs 1,000 followers). — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/are-instagram-trial-reels-worth-it); [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/how-instagram-trial-reels-work-72-hours/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via press] Skip rate and Retention chart in Reels Insights (around August 2025):**
  - Skip rate is "the percentage of views from people who skipped during the first 3 seconds" and replaces the old View Rate.
  - Meta labels it as estimated and still in development.
  - — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/); [Babbleboxx](https://www.babbleboxx.com/post/instagram-adds-reels-retention-skip-rate-what-influencer-marketers-should-do-next)
  - An April 2026 Insights redesign added share-rate and skip-rate percentages per post. — [Pulse.bot / Social Media Today](https://www.pulse.bot/digital-marketing/news/instagram-improves-insights-ui-adds-new-metrics-e6cd8e50-d191-4eec-ba5b-f57a4bfc60b1/)
- **[OFFICIAL, secondary quote, 04.10.2024] Mosseri:** "The first three seconds of a reel are super important." The same site notes that the "3 seconds" figure comes from Meta's ad-counting threshold (a "3-second video play"), not from a study of hooks. — [Inro hook generator](https://www.inro.social/tools/instagram-hook-generator)

### Inferences
- In 2025–2026 the official ranking model for **non-follower** reach rests on (a) how long people watch, (b) whether they send the reel in DMs, and (c) likes. Comments and saves are not in Mosseri's top three. For a channel whose whole funnel is "write in the comments", comments are a funnel step, not a lever for reach.
- Skip rate is the diagnostic that best explains failure in the first 3 seconds, even though it is not an official "signal". Three-second holds feed directly into watch time and the "watch all the way through" prediction.
- The 2026 Q3 statement that Reels are ranked as **sequences** suggests a reel is judged partly by whether it keeps a session going. That is a further reason a fast swipe-away is costly. (Inference; Meta gave no mechanism.)
- **Possible AI-content risk for this project.** The 31.08.2026 rule targets unlabeled *AI-generated people* profiles. Our cat is an AI-drawn cartoon, so it is probably not covered, but whether Instagram auto-applies an "AI info" label to GPT-Image drawings, and whether that affects reach, is unknown (see Gaps).
- **Possible originality risk for this project.** Reusing the same drawings or near-identical cuts across reels, or re-posting a trial variant whose content barely differs, could be read as "already posted on Instagram". The project already has a no-repeat rule (`check_originality`), which fits the official guidance.

### Gaps
- No official numeric weights (sends vs likes vs watch time), and no official threshold for a "good" watch time or skip rate.
- Not confirmed from a primary source whether Instagram's official Trial Reels eligibility in late 2026 is 1,000 followers or lower.
- The Meta Transparency Center Reels "system card" URL returned 404. Its current list of predictions could not be checked.
- Whether drawn AI images get auto-labeled "AI info", and whether that label changes distribution: no source found.

## 2. How initial test distribution works, and what a 100–200 view ceiling indicates

### Takeaway
Instagram has officially described distribution only qualitatively. A reel goes to a small group first, the top performers go to a slightly wider audience, and so on. There is **no published batch size or time window**. Specific "first test pool" numbers online are marketer folklore. A ceiling of 100–200 views on an account whose reels **do** reach non-followers points to content that fails the first stage on engagement (early skips, low average watch time, ~0 sends). An account-level block looks different: non-follower reach near zero and a warning in Account Status.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, OLDER — 2024] Staged expansion:** "the top performing reels are shown to a slightly wider audience, then the best of these are shown to an even wider group, and so on." — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)
- **Staged expansion, secondary summary of Mosseri:** a post is first shown to a relatively small group, early engagement (watch time, saves, shares) is evaluated, and reach expands if the response is positive. — [Pitchonnet](https://www.pitchonnet.com/pitch-feature/how-instagram-ranks-posts-adam-mosseri-breaks-down-the-algorithm-38374.html)
- **[MARKETER, unverified] Window claims:** a 30–60 min test window, a 1–4 h signal window and a 48 h cutoff all circulate without primary sourcing. — [search summary of miraflow.ai and likepropanel](https://miraflow.ai/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026-how-to-get-more-views)
- **[OFFICIAL] No shadowban; there is recommendation eligibility.** Instagram has announced no shadowban mechanism. Its announced system is recommendation eligibility, shown in Account Status. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
  - Ineligible content still reaches followers but not non-followers.
  - Account Status path: Settings → Account → Account Status.
  - It shows flagged items, and the creator can request a re-review.
  - — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators); [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/653964212890722); [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/instagram-account-status/)
- **[OFFICIAL] Eligibility ≠ distribution:** "eligibility for recommendations doesn't guarantee that your content will be recommended." — [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/653964212890722)
- **[MARKETER]** A missing "Trial" toggle can signal that the account is not eligible. — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/why-trial-reels-get-no-views)
- **[OFFICIAL, OLDER — 2023] Popularity signals:** follower count and engagement are considered "to help find compelling content from a wide array of people and give everyone a chance to find their audience." — [Instagram blog](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **[MARKETER]** Non-follower reach is "structurally slower and smaller" than warm follower reach. — [Postfa.st on Trial Reels](https://postfa.st/blog/instagram-trial-reels)

### Inferences
- **What the symptoms suggest.** The reels *do* reach non-followers, so the account and its content are almost certainly eligible: an eligibility block would show as near-zero non-follower views plus an Account Status flag. 100–200 views plus "a few likes and no clicks" is consistent with the first small batch rejecting the reel. That means a high 3-second skip rate, short average watch time relative to length, and sends per reach ≈ 0. With no strong positive signal, the next, wider batch is never unlocked. (Inference from the staged-expansion description; Instagram publishes no batch size, so "100–200" cannot be tied to an official pool size.)
- **Why so stable.** The same ceiling across many reels with different topics suggests a common cause in the first seconds or in the format (opening frame, voice, look), rather than topic choice. A common format-level cause would give similar first-batch results every time.
- **Two other possible contributors to check:**
  1. A thin interest graph for a niche of Russian-language viewers (section 5).
  2. Recommendation classifiers reading the reel as "majority text" or low quality. Our layout has a large caption band and the picture covers 75 % of the frame, so this is probably fine, but it is worth checking that on-screen text never dominates.
- **Diagnostic split:**
  - Low reach + high skip rate → the hook or first frame fails.
  - Low skip rate + low average watch time → the middle sags.
  - Good retention + zero sends → nothing is worth forwarding.
  - Near-zero non-follower share or an Account Status flag → eligibility problem.

### Gaps
- No official or credible measured data on the typical size of the first test batch for a small account. Numbers like "first 100–300 viewers" are folklore.
- How long Instagram keeps re-testing a reel (late "second wind") has no official documentation.

## 3. Credible data on length, hook timing, on-screen text, audio, posting frequency, captions, and benchmark numbers

### Takeaway
The best 2026 dataset (Socialinsider, 9.77 M Reels, Jan–Jun 2026) shows **45–60 s Reels with the highest median views (10,374) and engagement (0.35 %)**. Reels under 30 s got under half that median (4,700), and Reels over 3 min drop sharply. Buffer (2.1 M posts) finds 3–5 posts/week gives +12 % reach per post vs 1–2/week. Reels engagement benchmarks are low, about 0.3–0.5 % of followers, and reach for 1K–5K accounts is about 9.8 % of followers per Reel. No primary study on trending vs original audio exists; the "30–50 %/2.5×" claims are unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **[MARKETER, strong dataset] Socialinsider Reels length study** (published 14.07.2026, updated 20.10.2026; 9,771,822 organic Reels from brands with 1K–1M followers, Jan–Jun 2026). — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)

  | Length | Engagement rate | Median views |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0–30 s | 0.28 % | 4,700 |
  | 30–45 s | 0.30 % | 8,564 |
  | 45–60 s | 0.35 % | 10,374 |
  | 60–90 s | 0.30 % | 9,790 |
  | 90–120 s | 0.30 % | 8,000 |
  | 120–180 s | 0.33 % | 9,000 |
  | 180 s+ | 0.15 % | 4,428 |

  - Engagement rate = (likes + comments) / followers.
  - The article says Reels over 3 min are not placed in the Reels tab and not recommended to non-followers. Reels can be up to 20 min (Help Center).
  - The sample is brands, so it is biased toward accounts with existing audiences.
- **[OFFICIAL]** In January 2025 the maximum Reels length was extended to 3 minutes. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-length)
- **[MARKETER] Socialinsider 2026 benchmarks:**
  - Reels engagement was 0.48 % in Q2 2026, down from 0.50 % in Q1, declining two quarters in a row. — [Socialinsider benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/instagram-benchmarks)
  - A secondary site cites ~0.52 % for Reels vs 0.55 % for carousels. The methods conflict with the length study's 0.28–0.35 %, probably because of different samples and formulas.
- **[MARKETER] Reach rate:** accounts with 1,000–5,000 followers averaged a 9.78 % Reel reach rate (vs 8.80 % carousels, 7 % images), falling toward ~5 % for accounts near 1 M (140,000 Reels from business pages, Jan–Jun 2026, as cited). — [Socialinsider via search summary](https://www.socialinsider.io/instagram-benchmarks); [Inmagic](https://inmagic.ai/instagram-reels-statistics/)
  - Conflicting third-party figures: 3.2 % ER and 14–20 % reach. — [OwlClaw](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/instagram-reels-benchmarks) (unverified)
- **[MARKETER] Buffer posting frequency** (published 13.08.2025; 2.1 M posts from 102K accounts; each account compared with its own baseline): vs 1–2 posts/week, 3–5/week gives +12 % reach per post, 6–9/week +18 %, 10+/week +24 %. 3–5/week is the recommended "sweet spot". — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/study-shows-posting-more-instagram-leads-to-more-reach/757633/)
- **[MARKETER, OLDER — 2024] Buffer format study** (4 M posts): Reels reach 2.25× single images and 1.36× carousels (secondary report). — [Teleprompter](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/2025-instagram-reels-statistics)
- **[OFFICIAL] Hook timing:** "Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds." — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
  - Unofficial benchmark: ~50 % of viewers drop in the first 3 s; aim for a ≥ 60 % three-second hold. Sources stress that a "good" skip rate depends on niche and length. — [search summary: TrueFutureMedia](https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/lower-instagram-reels-skip-rate); [Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-hook-formulas)
- **[OFFICIAL] On-screen text:** keep text from covering most of the screen. "Majority text" reels are shown less to non-followers. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators); [Instagram blog 2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **[OFFICIAL, OLDER — 2023] Audio:** "go to the audio page" is one of the top predictions, and muted reels are demoted. — [Instagram blog](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **[MARKETER, unverified] Trending vs original audio:**
  - "+30–50 % reach" (attributed to HubSpot); not located. — [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/social-media-terms/trending-audio)
  - "Up to 2.5× views" (attributed to Hootsuite); not located.
  - A Kontentino Q&A says that using audio other creators also use does not reduce reach. — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/trending-audio-instagram-reach/)
  - No primary study from Later, Metricool or Socialinsider was found.
- **[OFFICIAL] Hashtags:** capped at 5 per post (18.12.2025); Instagram has contradicted the idea that hashtags boost reach. Keywords in handle, bio and caption help Search. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/); [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- **[OFFICIAL] Google indexing:** since 10.07.2025, public professional accounts' content is Google-indexable by default, so caption text has some search value. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)

### Inferences
- Our reels run ~30–40 s. In the Socialinsider data that is the 30–45 s band (median 8,564), close to the best band. Length is very unlikely to be the cause of a 100–200 ceiling: the gap between bands is about 2×, while our gap is about 50×.
- With engagement benchmarks this low (~0.3–0.5 % of followers), "a few likes" on 150 views (≈ 2–3 %) is not abnormal per view. The problem is that the reel never gets enough views; likes per reach are fine.
- Posting frequency matters only modestly (+12–24 % per post). It cannot explain a ceiling.
- Captions and subtitles: no credible measured effect on Reels reach was found. The official guidance only warns against text dominating the screen.

### Gaps
- No credible measured study (2025–2026) on subtitles or on-screen captions vs reach.
- No primary study on trending vs original audio.
- No official benchmark for average watch time; Socialinsider reported no watch-time data.
- No data split for Russian-language or CIS accounts in any benchmark study.

## 4. Which Insights metrics to check, and the thresholds cited

### Takeaway
Per Reel, check:
1. **Skip rate** (share of views skipped in the first 3 s) and the **Retention chart**.
2. **Average watch time** relative to reel length.
3. **Sends/shares per reach**.
4. **Likes per reach**.
5. **Views from non-followers** (share of reach).
6. **Account Status** (eligibility for recommendations).

Official thresholds don't exist. Marketers quote: hold ≥ 60 % past 3 s (skip ≤ 40 %), with "about half skipping" as average.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** Mosseri: "pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach." — [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Check Insights for how much of your reach comes from non-followers and how it changes over time. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- **[OFFICIAL, via press] Skip rate and Retention chart:**
  - Skip rate = % of views skipped in the first 3 s, labeled "estimated / in development".
  - The Retention chart's horizontal axis is seconds of the video and its vertical axis is % still watching, starting at 100 %.
  - — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992); [Babbleboxx](https://www.babbleboxx.com/post/instagram-adds-reels-retention-skip-rate-what-influencer-marketers-should-do-next)
- **[MARKETER] Qualitative bands (Metricool):**
  - About half skipping is average; few skipping is strong.
  - A sharp early drop on the retention chart means a weak hook.
  - "No official Instagram source defines a good skip rate."
  - — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/)
- **[MARKETER] Numeric targets:** ~50 % drop in the first 3 s is typical; aim for ≥ 60 % three-second hold. — [TrueFutureMedia](https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/lower-instagram-reels-skip-rate)
- **[MARKETER] Reading skip rate with watch time:** a low skip rate with poor watch time means "the hook worked but the middle sagged". Test hooks by changing only the first 2–3 s, ideally as separate Trial Reels. — [search summary: Async](https://async.com/blog/reel-retention-tips/); [Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-hook-formulas)

### Inferences
- **A practical diagnosis table for the owner, filled from Insights for the last 10–15 reels:** skip rate, average watch time ÷ length, % views from non-followers, sends, likes, Account Status.
  - Skip rate ≥ 50 % on most reels → the opening (first frame, first words, motion) fails *for strangers*, even if it seems strong to us.
  - Skip rate fine but average watch time < ~40–50 % of length → the middle loses people.
  - Sends ≈ 0 → there is no "I need to send this to X" moment.
  - Non-follower share high but views low → eligible, but losing the first test. Non-follower share low → check Account Status.
  - The ~40–50 % watch-time cut-off is my working heuristic, not a sourced benchmark.
- **The Retention chart is the key instrument.** Where the curve falls (0–1 s, 1–3 s, mid-reel) maps directly onto our pipeline's parts: first frame and fly-in, the hook phrase, the second hook, and so on.

### Gaps
- No official numeric benchmarks for skip rate, average watch time or sends per reach.
- Whether Insights' "views from non-followers" for a Russian-language account reflects VPN geolocation correctly is unknown.

## 5. Russian-language Instagram audience in 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Instagram has been blocked in Russia since 2022, and Meta is designated "extremist". **Advertising on Instagram became illegal in Russia from 01.09.2025**. Mediascope stopped including Instagram in its ratings from 2025, so audience figures are estimates. The remaining audience is smaller, VPN-dependent and **~81 % female** (Brand Analytics). It is partly passive, and many bloggers moved to Telegram. There are no measured data on Reels reach for Russian-language accounts specifically.

### Cited Findings
- **Audience size:** an estimated 20–30 M monthly users via VPN vs ~80 M before the block (an estimate without a primary source). — [vkusnovk.ru](https://vkusnovk.ru/blog/auditoriya-instagram-v-rossii); [elama.ru](https://elama.ru/blog/socialnye-seti-v-rossii/)
- **Daily reach:** Instagram's daily reach grew 19 %, to 7.4 M people (an ad-group estimate via Kommersant; period not stated). — [Forbes.ru](https://www.forbes.ru/tekhnologii/564147-v-rossii-sokratilas-auditoria-telegram-i-vyrosli-ohvaty-instagram-i-discord)
- **Mediascope:** stopped including Instagram in its ratings from 2025. The last cited daily figure is 6.8 M (Feb 2024, 12+). — [ppc.world](https://ppc.world/articles/auditoriya-devyati-krupneyshih-socsetey-v-rossii-v-2024-godu-issledovaniya-i-cifry/)
- **Survey (TASS, 2025):** 32 % deleted their accounts, 28 % became inactive, 26 % log in very rarely, 3 % stay active. — [ppc.world](https://ppc.world/articles/auditoriya-devyati-krupneyshih-socsetey-v-rossii-v-2024-godu-issledovaniya-i-cifry/)

### Inferences
- **A smaller and more scattered audience pool.** The pool of active Russian-language Reels viewers is much smaller and more fragmented than before 2022: VPN users in Russia plus diaspora and CIS viewers. The recommendation engine pairs content with viewers by interests and language, so a Russian-language productivity/ADHD niche has fewer matching viewers per test batch. That can lower the ceiling, but it does not explain one this low (100–200) on its own. Other Russian-language creators still reach tens of thousands of views, as the 19 M active authors suggest.
- **A female-heavy audience.** With ~81 % women, hooks, characters and examples should fit that audience. This is a content-fit point, not an algorithm rule.
- **VPN geolocation is a hypothesis.** VPN exit countries (e.g. Netherlands, Finland) may make Russian-speaking viewers look like users in other countries. Russian-language reels might then be tested on audiences in those countries, many of whom don't speak Russian, which would mean early skips. I found no source confirming or refuting this. It is plausible, but language signals (interface language, past watching) probably dominate.
- **Russian-language reels with subtitles.** Burned-in subtitles plus clear visuals help the reel survive a test batch that contains non-Russian speakers (an inference from the point above).
